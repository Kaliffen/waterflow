Consolidating a finished eleven-record store moved exactly one record, because
under the shipped mapping almost nothing is a watermark.

Run on waterflowtest2's crowd-sim subject, which is finished: nine items closed,
none open. Before, eleven records. After, one in history, nine live, and one new
goal. The clearing is a tenth of what the design implies.

**Why.** `slice` is the only atom that maps to `watermark`. A `prove` verdict is
a fact, a `prove` measurement and a `critique` are observations, `seam` is a
fact, `define` is an idiom -- so the records that read most like work traces
survive by construction. That may be correct: a critique's findings are worth
keeping, and a passing suite is a fact. But the owner's intent was to remove
work traces and keep facts, and on this evidence the fold removes one kind of
trace out of four.

**Two superseded prove records stayed.** 7f31 and 9d44 are spent by any reading
-- something replaced them -- and they sit in the retrieval directory excluded
by convention alone, which is exactly the weakness that moving watermarks was
meant to fix. The fold says nothing about them. They are more numerous than
watermarks in every store I have looked at, and they are the clearer case: a
superseded record is definitionally not current.

**The survivors carry no `kind`, and consolidation is what was supposed to fix
that.** The fold deliberately does not rewrite survivors, which is right -- a
fact nothing re-ran is not made truer by being restated. But 2026-08-29-a1d8
reasons that consolidation is what retires kind-less records as subjects finish,
and this run shows it does not: nine records came through the fold and every one
of them still has to be classified by atom on every read. The goal as written
cannot be reached by the mechanism it names.

Neither finding says the ontology is wrong. The goal it produced is the best
thing in this run. They say the fold is narrower than the idea that motivated
it, and that the two obvious extensions -- superseded records to history, and
kinds settled at consolidation -- are where its value actually is.
