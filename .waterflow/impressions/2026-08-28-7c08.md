Critique's two axes are fan-out branches: the contradiction rule returns to
`critique` as a pointer at topology.md, and the escalation raised in
2026-08-28-2e6f is closed in that direction.

Settled by the owner. The spec axis had argued the rule was deleted on a
justification that existed in no record -- that the axes are deliberately not
independent -- and had followed it to the real consequence: topology.md legalises
`fan-out` only over independent questions, so a `critique` whose axes are not
independent is running a shape the reference does not sanction. Restoring the
pointer answers that. Standards and Spec are independent questions, the fan-out
is legal, and two axes disagreeing on the same hunk is a finding to be surfaced
rather than an average to be taken.

What stays separate is presentation. "Do not merge or re-rank across axes" is
about the report and remains `critique`'s own rule; reconciling a contradiction
is topology's and is now pointed at. The two were being conflated, which is how
the rule came to be deleted rather than rehomed.
