An anchor that predates the work it describes is caught on both sides: `land`
re-anchors the records it proved to the integrated revision, and `recall`
reports a record whose whole scope was created after its anchor as freshness
unknown rather than stale.

Settled by the owner after the second field run. 2026-08-28-f2b6 named the case
where no commit exists and left the commoner one: a commit does exist, `HEAD`
resolves, the ancestry check passes, and the record still points at a tree where
its subject is absent. Five of nine live records in that run were in this state
and read as ordinary drift.

The owner rejected fixing only one side. Land alone leaves every store true only
after integration and lying throughout the build, which is the state the field
run spent its whole life in. Recall alone never makes the store accurate, it
only warns forever, and it charges every future reader for a defect that could
have been repaired once.

This is the same shape as the conditions decision in 2026-08-28-d5c8, and the
same half was missing again: the write side was assumed and the read side was
where the value actually reached the next agent. Worth noticing as a pattern
rather than as two incidents.
