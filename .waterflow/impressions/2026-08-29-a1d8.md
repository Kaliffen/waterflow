Every live record in this store declares its `kind`, decided by counting records
with no `kind:` line; the target is zero and it gates whether Waterflow can
claim the ontology is demonstrated rather than described.

Currently 32 of 38. Every one of them predates the field, so none is a mistake,
and both readers that matter -- the proof gate and `recall` -- classify them by
atom instead. The design is therefore compatible with this store. It is not
demonstrated by it.

That distinction is the reason this is a goal rather than an observation. The
gap is not a defect to be fixed by hand: consolidation is what retires these
records, subject by subject, as work finishes. Backfilling `kind` across
thirty-two landed records would be the curation the store's first page forbids,
and would put a field on records nobody re-derived.

AGENTS.md says a record left behind after the code moved is worse here than
anywhere, because this is where the contract is supposed to be demonstrated.
This goal is that sentence made countable.

It carries no `atom` because no atom emits a goal. That is also what it is for:
this is the first goal in any Waterflow store, and writing it is the check that
one can be written at all.
