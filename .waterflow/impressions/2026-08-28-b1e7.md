Every dispatched agent is joined at or before the boundary that spawned it, and
dispatch is illegal without a named deliverable, budget, and join point.

Owner reported agents that kept running during a build of a crowd-sim app. The
cause was that topology.md described only how to *choose* a shape and never the
return leg, and `dig` explicitly said to dispatch and keep working with no
instruction to collect. When a join point arrives with nothing back there are
exactly two moves and the flow says which it took: wait, or stop the agent and
name what is now unproven. Leaving it running was rejected as a third option --
it is the failure being fixed.

Two containment rules were settled with it: a dispatched agent does not itself
dispatch unless its brief names a ceiling, and an agent that finds adjacent work
reports it rather than starting it.
