Writer: anything that changes files in a working tree -- a session, a subagent,
or a person at a keyboard. One tree admits one writer at a time.

Avoid: "agent" for this role. The word was doing double duty, naming both a
dispatched subagent and any party that edits, and the narrower reading is what
let two peer sessions collide in one checkout while the rule read as satisfied.
"Concurrent access" is also avoided: it describes the symptom and names nothing
that can be counted or serialized.

The term is defined because the constraint is stated in terms of it. A rule
about writers is checkable -- count them -- where a rule about agents silently
exempts the commonest case.
