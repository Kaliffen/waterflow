Five slices in a chain with one genuine parallel pair, ending in a real
consolidation of the store the design came from.

The shape is a chain because almost everything here reads the contract, and two
slices that both edit it are a merge conflict arranged in advance. The one honest
parallel pair is 2d5a and 3e6b: the gate and the contract on one side, `recall`
alone on the other, disjoint paths and no edge between them.

The first slice carries both the contract change and the gate's tolerance of it,
deliberately. A `kind` field that nothing reads is not a tracer bullet, and
splitting them would publish a slice that cannot be observed at the seam.

The last slice is in another repository, waterflowtest2. It is kept in this
backlog because it is the only slice that can fail for the reason the whole
design might be wrong: the knowledge might read worse after consolidation than
the eleven records it replaces, and no fixture in this repository can discover
that. It is the tracer bullet for the idea rather than for the mechanism.

Two questions were put to the owner and not answered, so the published shape
takes the conservative reading of both: 4f7c stays blocked by 3e6b as well as
2d5a, because consolidation has to know what survives, and 5a8d stays in this
backlog rather than being deferred to the other repository's own flow.
