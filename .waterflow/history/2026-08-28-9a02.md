Atoms end with a one-line report and composites with a fixed-shape markdown
block; five emoji markers carry status and no ANSI colour is used.

Owner asked for "coloured and structured feedback" after finding the flow weak
at saying what it had done. Colour was resolved to markdown rather than escape
codes because the terminal renders GFM and an ANSI sequence is either invisible
or literal. Structure was resolved to a per-step block over a live running
trace, and over doing both, to keep the prose budget in each skill small.

The block carries a row per atom including skipped ones, the worst outcome
spelled out below the table, a footer naming what persisted, and the count of
agents dispatched and joined -- the last being the reporting half of the
lifecycle rule in [[agent-lifecycle]].
