The fold could not be followed in any store that exists: a query for
`kind: watermark` would have moved zero records and reported success, because
almost everything anywhere predates the field.

Two axes, both joined. Fixed at 531bcd2.

**Spec's finding, and the important one.** Thirty-two of this repository's
thirty-six records carry no `kind`, including the only genuine watermark. The
kinds table existed but was framed write-side -- which atom writes which kind --
and never as a way to read a legacy record. The gate had already solved the same
problem for itself and said so in one sentence; the fold had no equivalent. The
failure would have been silent, which is the worst available shape: a step that
reports success having done nothing.

**Standards' finding, and the structural one.** `land` had acquired two
store-writing steps that also carry judgement, each defended with "no atom owns
it". That argument works every time, so it justifies anything, and a third
exception was due next slice. The rule was amended to name the two writes
integration may perform. Changing a documented rule deliberately is the honest
move; accreting past it three times is not.

**Two smaller ones, both real.** The trigger fired on any subject with no open
items, but an empty frontier means "never tracked" as often as "finished", and
four subjects in this store have never had an item. And the fold claimed facts
are re-anchored by the staleness rule -- which re-anchors by superseding, and
would have fabricated facts nothing re-ran.

**On the proof.** Spec argued that closing this slice on `npm test` overstates
what was checked, since none of the eighteen fixtures touches consolidation.
That is correct and the slice is closed on the critique instead, which proof.md
admits as a proof kind. It is the weaker of the two seams named in
2026-08-29-e7d4, and naming it as the proof is the point of having named it as a
seam.
