# Align

Run these atoms in order. Do not restate their rules here.

1. **`recall`** the subject. Open by saying what is already settled, and mark
   anything stale.
2. **`interrogate`** the gaps. Rounds until the frontier is empty.
3. **`define`** the terms that came up ambiguous and the decisions that are hard
   to reverse.

**Done when** the owner confirms shared understanding, and every answer that
will matter next loop exists as a record rather than as a memory of the
conversation.

Next: `shape`, unless the work is small enough to slice inline.

**Report** the block before moving on:
[reporting.md](../waterflow/references/reporting.md).
